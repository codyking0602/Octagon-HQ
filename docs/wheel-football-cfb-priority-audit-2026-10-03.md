# Wheel of Football — 2026 CFB candidate-priority audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-03  
**Programs manually reviewed:** 68 / 68  
**Scope:** SEC 16, Big Ten 18, Big 12 16, ACC 17, Notre Dame 1  
**Result:** 65 programs changed from the generated Step 2 baseline; 180 roster-slot groups changed. Minnesota, California, and NC State were unchanged after review.  
**Status:** Candidate selection/order audit complete. Player grading is intentionally not part of this audit.

## Locked football-quality philosophy

The Step 2 Ourlads/ESPN baseline was only a starting structure. Step 3 converts it into Octagon-owned current-player priority.

The audit does **not** treat raw ESPN roster order, raw depth-chart row order, recruiting stars, fantasy value, draft projection, fame, or any external rating system as football importance.

Current depth/role, established current-team value, official conference/team recognition, 2026 completed-game production, transfers, injuries/availability, and position fit were reviewed together. Production is evidence, not an automatic ranking formula.

Temporary injury or suspension does not automatically remove an established player who remains current team property. A departed player should not remain eligible. Candidate groups are not padded with random backups just to hit a quota.

## Candidate shape after audit

- QB: normally 1; max 2 only for a real current competition or meaningful two-QB situation. All 68 audited programs remain at one QB.
- RB: 2–3.
- WR: 3–4.
- TE: 1–2 source candidates for Flex construction.
- Flex: exactly 4 meaningful RB/WR/TE options.
- Front Seven: 5–6 football-sensible DL/EDGE/LB candidates.
- Secondary: 5–6 football-sensible CB/S candidates.
- Head Coach: 1.

## Audit result by conference

### SEC

The SEC audit restored or elevated current-impact names where the source baseline understated football importance. Examples include Yhonzae Pierre at Alabama; Vernell Brown III and Dallas Wilson at Florida; Raylen Wilson, Elijah Griffin, and Chris Cole at Georgia; Ahmad Hardy and Daeden Hopkins at Missouri; David Stone and Kip Lewis at Oklahoma; Dylan Stewart at South Carolina; Colin Simmons and Rasheem Biles at Texas; and Terry Bussey at Texas A&M.

### Big Ten

The Big Ten audit corrected current skill-position and defensive priorities while retaining established players through temporary availability issues where appropriate. Examples include L.J. Phillips Jr. at Iowa; Riley Pettijohn at Ohio State; A'Mauri Washington and Matayo Uiagalelei at Oregon; Benjamin Brahmer at Penn State; and expanded current-impact groups at Indiana, Michigan, Nebraska, USC, Washington, and Wisconsin.

### Big 12

The Big 12 audit emphasized current role and 2026 impact rather than preseason/source-row ordering. Examples include Cooper Blomstrom at Arizona; Keanu Tanuvasa and Cade Uluave at BYU; Malcolm Simmons at Texas Tech; Trey Reynolds at Utah; and current skill-position reordering at Arizona State, Colorado, Houston, Iowa State, Kansas State, Oklahoma State, TCU, and West Virginia.

### ACC

The ACC audit added current-impact contributors and corrected position grouping. Examples include E'Lla Boykin at Boston College; Malachi Toney and Ahmad Moten at Miami; Xay Davis at Virginia; and current production-driven additions across Georgia Tech, Louisville, Pittsburgh, Stanford, Syracuse, Virginia Tech, and Wake Forest.

Three important classification corrections are locked: Pittsburgh linebacker Braylan Lovelace is not a Secondary candidate; Texas Tech linebacker John Curry is not a Secondary candidate; Duke linebacker Luke Mergott is not a Secondary candidate.

### Notre Dame

Notre Dame's reviewed front seven is led by Kyngstonn Viliamu-Asa, Bryce Young, and Boubacar Traore. Leonard Moore leads the reviewed secondary group.

## Step 2 reconciliation warnings

The generated baseline retained source-divergence warnings when Ourlads and ESPN did not expose a uniquely matching current name at the same moment. Step 3 reviewed those warnings in football context rather than silently deleting the player or trusting one provider's row order.

Those warnings remain attached to the Step 2 provenance data so future maintenance can see exactly where source reconciliation was imperfect. They are not player grades and they are not a separate gameplay ranking.

## Runtime boundary

The audited priority file is now the candidate-selection authority for later CFB Wheel work. The generated Step 2 baseline remains intact as provenance for future diffs and maintenance.

The CFB Wheel is **not yet grading-complete or gameplay-launch-ready**. Step 4 must grade only this audited survivor population using the locked current-ability grading philosophy.
