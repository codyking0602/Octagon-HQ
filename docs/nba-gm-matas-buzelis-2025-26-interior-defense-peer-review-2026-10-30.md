# NBA GM — Matas Buzelis interior-defense peer calibration

**October 30, 2026. Unreleased editorial review; one narrow skill adjustment proposed. All six skills and playable position are NOT independently certified.**

## Evidence

- The 2025–26 NBA regular season provides a representative workload: **77 GP, 29.2 MPG, 116 blocks / 1.5 BPG and 4.5% block rate**. His 2024–25 sample was 80 GP with 75 blocks / 0.9 BPG (4.3% block rate). These are supported by [Basketball-Reference seasonal data](https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/buzelma01.html) and [FOX NBA defense season history](https://www.foxsports.com/nba/matas-buzelis-player-stats?category=defense&seasonType=reg).
- [2025–26 NBA tracking-based shot defense](https://www.therundown.gg/research/nba/player/matas-buzelis?gt=Regular+Season&season=2025-26) attributes **6.1 defended rim shots/game**, **57.7% allowed at rim**, **5.9 percentage points below those shooters' normal rim accuracy**, and **5.2 two-point contests/game**. This is direct position-relevant evidence of weak-side interior help, not merely counting total blocks. A [separate NBA-defense-dashboard mirror](https://nba.highlightfactory.app/team/chicago-bulls/matas-buzelis) reports 455 defender-nearest attempts within six feet, 58.0% made versus those opponents' 63.2% baseline (−5.2 points). Caveat: tracking's nearest-defender attribution is not the same as individual responsibility on every possession.
- Counterweight: his NBA [2025–26 defensive box plus/minus was −0.4](https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/buzelma01.html) and [lineup-adjusted rim deterrence was −1.2 extra rim attempts/100, with +1.2 points/100 rim protection](https://spreadsheetsports.com/players/matas-buzelis/). His interior help and shot interference are better than his overall team-defense impact; the evidence **does not** make him a high-80s center-style paint anchor. The cited public tracking site's 78 starts versus 77 GP line is internally inconsistent and was *not* used.
