# Average Fan UFC upper-grade editorial decision

**Retired:** every generated `average-fan:ufc:g5:*:title-identity` candidate.

These questions ask players to infer the fighter from the **exact date** and result of a title fight, with another opponent appended. On a hard question, precise date trivia is not itself a recognizable sporting achievement and may yield a needlessly difficult identity test. Keep higher-grade UFC questions grounded in recognizable opponent combinations, title bouts, outcomes, and verified fighters instead. Reducing bank inventory is preferable to keeping weak exact-date riddles.

This filter affects only **future question selection**. Existing published setups contain immutable private evidence and are not rewritten. Validate selected Grade 5 inventory and future official Daily simulations before release.
