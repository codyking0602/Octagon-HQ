# NBA Higher or Lower: 24 portrait restorations

The NBA headshot endpoint served identical gray silhouettes for 24 older players.
This restoration uses article-identified player photographs for 23 people from
Wikimedia Commons and the Terry Cummings player photograph from the National
Basketball Retired Players Association.

The game includes photographer attribution, image source and license links
under the unobtrusive "Historical photo credits" disclosure for displayed
players. Images establish identity only; game season and team affiliation
remain determined by verified stat data, never by photo year or uniform.

The exact-PR-head remote audit fetches all **184** eligible player photo assets,
rejecting duplicate/silhouette/unreadable assets before merge.
