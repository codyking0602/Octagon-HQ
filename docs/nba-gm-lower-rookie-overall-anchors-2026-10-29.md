## Standards enforced

- **71–74 is a credible NBA current-skill band, not a verdict on future talent.** Do not give every recent high draft pick an 80 based on expectations.
- Restrict assessments to demonstrated pro/college abilities and current role, with very low confidence while the sample is small; NBA 2K27 is not a starting number.
- **Aday Mara** earns a higher initial rating than some older second-round players because of visible rim protection and rotation usage, not merely because he was drafted No. 12.
- **Trevon Brazile**'s good first-week rebounding does not make him a strong starter already. Do not project his early stats for 82 games.
- For fully established veterans, 70–74 can mean limited NBA current ability too; draft pedigree is not required for inclusion. The final 330-person population must be confirmed team-by-team and include other true depth players.
