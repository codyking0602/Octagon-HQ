# NFL GM — fast biweekly maintenance (2026-10-28)

This is a **small maintenance system** for current NFL ability in GM. The priority is accuracy without turning the game into a scouting department. NFL only; do not change UFC or CFB.

## Cadence and division of work

- GitHub Actions: every other Tuesday at 16:45 Central, starting with the existing October 27, 2026 anchor. Reuse the canonical `wheel-nfl-biweekly-editorial.yml` workflow; no duplicate monitors.
- Madden: preserve genuine official EA OVR checkpoints. Compare to the **last scheduled 14-day checkpoint**; flag Madden OVR increases or decreases of **at least two** among the 594 NFL GM player-position identities. The prior EA Week 3 retrospective (300) is **not** a biweekly sample. Never allow a failed collector to overwrite baseline or report zero candidates.
- Quick triage: review **all Madden flags briefly**; prioritize 4+ OVR movement, 3+ with substantial current EA/HQ disagreement, or 2+ with very large EA/HQ disagreement for targeted football research. These are **workload signals, not grade formulas**. A flag that is quick-screened is not an approved hold. No standalone current EA/HQ gap, stat changes, snaps, injury, age or rumor may create another player-rating review candidate.
- Deeper football research: check the selected candidates' multiweek performance and efficiency, role and injury context, outside scouting analysis and **position-relative HQ peers**. Present increases, decreases and warranted holds. Explicitly carry forward identity/source blockers. No quota for deep reviews and no minimum or maximum number of approved changes.
- Coaches: independently review **all 32 every cycle**: actual current coaching decisions, scheme and play-calling, adjustments, staff/player development, and historical track record. A name/role match is **not** a coaching grade decision. Do not derive changes from wins alone.
- Roster/contracts: verify exceptions and independent transaction evidence only. Absence from one provider's roster is not proof of transfer. Ordinary unchanged rows require no additional work.
- Owner: receive **one short proposal** with old → proposed new HQ grades and concise rationales. Nothing changes without explicit approval. The issue and artifact generated by GitHub are **preparatory evidence and triage**, not automatically approved recommendations.
- Publication: apply accepted current HQ ratings in date-versioned append-only authority; preserve prior Wheel results, existing games, stable GM IDs and contracts. Require exact-head tests + backend migration verification, then merge and check production.
- Development: automatically reevaluate each HQ player whose newly published grade differs from the **previous saved published 594-player checkpoint**, not repeatedly from original October calibration. Independently screen aging-low-decline and newly drafted-player outlier profiles. Do not auto-edit development probability values; only propose evidence-backed amendments for approval. First upgraded workflow run **bootstraps the current authority** and produces zero new grade-change rechecks, because already-published grades were previously screened.

## Time budget — normal cycles

| Step | Editorial target |
|---|---:|
| Madden flag triage | 10 minutes |
| Selected player reviews | 15–20 minutes |
| 32 coach reviews | 15–20 minutes |
| Development exceptions | 10–15 minutes |
| Roster and contract exceptions, owner summary | 5 minutes |

Target **roughly one hour of editorial attention**, with evidence collection in advance. This is **not** a machine-enforced wall clock limit, and CI/deployment occur separately. Unresolved complex cases roll forward visibly instead of holding up every unrelated decision.

## Boundaries

Automated GitHub triage is deterministic; **it cannot substitute for independent football editorial research**. The owner approval issue may be opened in ChatGPT for the research and final proposed adjustment list. If a future AI reviewer is integrated, it must use verified source-grounded evidence, document each individual judgment, and never be granted direct automatic grading publication authority.

Monitoring/read-only issue generation and post-rating **reevaluation** can be automatic; **owner approval and probability modifications are not**.
