## Grading semantic

Grade **actual current college head-coaching ability right now**.

Current 2026 coaching performance and trajectory lead the evaluation, 2025 is a major stabilizer, and relevant multi-year head-coaching evidence keeps a small early-season sample from creating nonsense.

The conceptual weighting direction is judgment-led rather than formulaic:
- roughly 40–50% current 2026 performance / trajectory;
- roughly 25–30% 2025;
- roughly 20–30% relevant multi-year demonstrated head-coaching level.

For first-year coaches and coaches at new schools, inherited roster strength is separated from evidence attributable to the coach. Coordinator success is useful context but is not treated as proof of elite head-coaching ability.

Do **not** grade from AP rank, school prestige, recruiting ranking, raw roster talent, fame, career win total, old championships, future projection, or NFL reputation.
