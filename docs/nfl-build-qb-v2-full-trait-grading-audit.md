## Cross-trait notes

- Arm is functional physical throwing talent; touch/placement is not double-counted there.
- Accuracy is repeatable placement; completion percentage is evidence, not the grade.
- Processing is football recognition/decision quality, never an intelligence label.
- Mobility combines rushing threat, escape/second-reaction creation, and pocket movement.
- Overall is a simple four-trait average for audit convenience only. It does not control any trait grade.
- Generator tuning happens after grades are locked; the generator may select from grades but may not alter them.
