## Still not complete

Current first-pass inventory is **138 individually proposed names (100 + 38)**, not a 330-player bank. Lower 70–74 anchors are still **unfilled on purpose** until a verified pool of actual NBA depth players and recent draftees exists; manufacturing a weak rating just to reach a number would repeat the mistakes identified in the old NFL audit. Do not infer that an off-ball specialist's strong trait means an 85 overall, or that a rookie's draft investment implies an 80 floor.

Review outliers and confirm real player/team/position data before publishing. Grades may increase, decrease or be withheld after deep comparison. Never merge this editorial snapshot into public player authority.
