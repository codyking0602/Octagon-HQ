## Source-backed adjustments

- **Todd Monken 80→81:** Cleveland's five new offensive linemen and the revamped system created real early adjustment friction; Week 7 rush effort of 183 yards and three touchdowns supports a measured credit for sustained design and execution. [BrownsZone, October 28](https://www.brownszone.com/2026/10/28/run-game-comes-to-life-vs-titans-provides-hope-for-rest-of-season/).
- **Klint Kubiak 84→83:** His early 3-0 credibility keeps the grade strong for a first-year coach, but red-zone and third-down execution and inconsistent fourth-down decisions justify removing one point, not overreacting to team record. [Reuters, October 26](https://www.reuters.com/sports/raiders-streaking-wrong-way-will-stick-with-kirk-cousins-qb--flm-2026-10-26/) and [Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/raiders/what-we-learned-a-closer-look-at-the-raiders-first-loss-of-the-season-to-the-chiefs-3896407/).
- **John Harbaugh 88→89:** 4-2-1 Giants; rebuilt staff, offense adapted to QB transitions, established accountability culture. This is repeatable coaching value above adjacent good coaches but does not reach elite 90+. [NFL, October 27](https://www.nfl.com/news/how-john-harbaugh-put-on-a-coaching-masterclass-in-lead-up-to-giants-week-7-win).
- **Kevin O'Connell remains 89:** 6-0 Vikings, but defense allows 12.3 points per game (first) and offense scores 20.8 (23rd). Credit team leadership without inferring an elite offensive coaching leap. [Pro-Football-Reference 2026](https://www.pro-football-reference.com/teams/min/2026_advanced.htm).
