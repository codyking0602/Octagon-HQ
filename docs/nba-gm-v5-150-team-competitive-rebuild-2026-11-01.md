# NBA GM V5 — competitive-strength rebuild, November 1, 2026

**Scope: owner-only / private.** Exact purpose: correct the false 40-win middle-of-league interpretation of a Wembanyama/Barnes/Holiday/Bane/Bridges lineup, without inventing wins for every team or rewriting prior saved NBA GM runs. This is a `simulationModelVersion=5` opt-in at new V4 franchise creation. Historic V2/V3/V4 runs retain their original simulation outcomes. NFL GM and final 90% roster / 3% wins / 7% postseason score are unchanged.

## Source-backed layers, clear limitations

1. **All 150 real NBA team-seasons, 2021–22 through 2025–26:** audited Basketball-Reference unadjusted offense/defense, net rating and wins from `docs/nba-gm-150-team-season-efficiency-2022-2026.csv`; 150 exact bracket outcomes from `docs/nba-gm-150-team-season-postseason-2022-2026.csv`. Every season has 30 teams, 1,230 wins and 1,230 losses. The 2025–26 held-out relationship is **wins = 40.9983 + 2.2684 × observed net rating** (RMSE 3.339 wins). This measured relationship is *conditional on observed efficiency*, not a training fit for fictional player skills.
2. **Actual starter and bench playing time:** source [willccbb/nbabench-data 2023–24](https://github.com/willccbb/nbabench-data/blob/main/data/2024/season.jsonl) and [2024–25](https://github.com/willccbb/nbabench-data/blob/main/data/2025/season.jsonl), 30 teams per season. Sort player records by season total playing time (`gp × mpg`), count five top-minute players and next two; the 2024 cohort averages **30.6 starter MPG / 24.1 bench MPG** and 2025 averages **30.1 / 24.5**. This is a workload estimate, **not a claim that total-minute leaders were each official starters for all 82 games**. Seven-player representation uses five 30.2-MPG slots (0.63 share of 48) and two 24.0-MPG slots (0.50). The 0.85 remaining 48-minute-equivalent units are explicitly charged at replacement-level -2.0, rather than disappearing from the efficiency model. Model ignores injuries and exact game rotations.
3. **Player impact calibration anchor, not a 2022–26 historical rating series:** 2025–26 published [Basketball-Reference BPM leaders](https://www.basketball-reference.com/leagues/NBA_2026_leaders.html): Nikola Jokić 14.2, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander 11.7, Victor Wembanyama 10.7, Luka Dončić 9.3, Scottie Barnes 4.2, Anthony Edwards 4.5, among others. [BPM methodology](https://www.basketball-reference.com/about/bpm2.html): positional box-score impact is expressed per 100 possessions and team average is 0. The game translates the already-audited 330 **current ability** overalls to a bounded, piecewise **proxy** (99≈10, 95≈6.5, 90≈3.5, 85≈1.3, 80≈−0.2, 75≈−1.6, 70≈−3.2) and then uses role-specific shooting, creation, perimeter defense, rim defense and rebounding adjustments. **This mapping is an authored model; it is not fitted to time-consistent 150 historical player ability grades.** Skill adjustments are individually capped at ±1.0 BPM. The game does not assert true BPM for any player's NBA GM six-skill value.
4. **Small bounded complementarity, not fake base wins:** three or more starter shooters (≥80 shooting) help spacing (+0.35 net), fewer than two penalize it (−0.65); two legitimate strong creators (+0.25); a 90+ rim defender combined with an 80+ perimeter starting trio (+0.30). Full fit correction bounded [-1,+1] point/100. This preserves identity and testable components without arbitrary +3 net to every roster. Future work should fit interaction strengths on actual longitudinal five-year lineups; the current coefficients remain provisional.
5. **Best-of-seven postseason:** 150 outcomes show 80/150 main-bracket participants, 40 semifinalists, 20 conference finalists, 10 Finals teams and five champions. Main-bracket participation by observed wins: 0/31 (under 30), 2/26 (30–39), 11/24 (40–44), 28/30 (45–49), 41/41 (50+). For each of four playoff rounds, seed selects one real historical opponent that reached that round, including its observed net rating and wins. Seven seeded 2-2-1-1-1 home/away games determine advancement through a logistic efficiency contrast. Game-level logit scale 10.5 and home advantage 2.5 are still provisional parameters, not a fitted series prediction. No preordained title by win threshold; a 43-win champion is improbable, not impossible. *No 2021–26 NBA team under 50 wins won a title* in the five-year cohort.
6. **Preservation:** only `economyVersion=4` with `simulationModelVersion=5` opts into V5; existing incomplete or finished runs with no V5 marker still invoke their unmodified V2/V3/V4 season branch. New model does not change verified NBA salary windows, contract continuity, roster construction, trade behavior or scoring.

## Non-negotiable comparison and test coverage

- The owner's real **Year-1 opening rotation**: Jrue Holiday (PG), Desmond Bane (SG), Mikal Bridges (SF), Scottie Barnes (PF), Victor Wembanyama (C), Norman Powell (Guard), Cameron Boozer (Big). The prior model reported 45 wins and defense 74.5 despite Wembanyama's 99 interior defense and Barnes's 91 perimeter defense.
- The owner's real **post-trade rotation**: swap Scottie Barnes for Jalen Smith; account for development without modifying old recorded results. New model must reduce expectations for removing an elite versatile defender rather than pretending it makes no difference.
- Compare 50 deterministic three-season seeds each for these two actual rosters, a legal contender and a weak depth roster: 600 authoritative SQL season outcomes. Validate win ordering, strong defense, tournament variance, deterministic replay, all 82-game records, all 150 source rows and legacy branch isolation.
- Require full exact-head backend migration replay, source tests, frontend/typecheck and production readback before a merge. Keep NBA GM hidden/owner-only.

## Research still required for *predictive* certification

The V5 model meaningfully replaces an arbitrary offensive/defensive averaging formula with minutes/impact-informed rosters and actual opponent-based series. It is nevertheless not a fitted five-year **player/lineup** predictive model: 2022–26 contemporaneous individual box-impact, reliable rotation minutes by season, lineup composition, injuries, team four factors and chronology-safe skill ratings have **not** been assembled into a jointly trained model. Do not describe this as scientifically validated; owner test feedback and further research are required before public launch. The project should fit offensive and defensive effects separately with five-year snapshots and held-out 2025–26 metrics. No approval to publish NBA GM is implied.
