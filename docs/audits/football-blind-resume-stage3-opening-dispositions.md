- The six rewrites only reorder **existing** source-bound evidence dimensions; none fabricates, edits, or removes statistics.
- The 18 provisional keeps are **not** acceptance of all factual values: cross-era sources, coaching records and season stats still need independent primary-source checks as part of Stage 3.
- The post-review scoring ladder, hard/hard/medium slate shape, mixed NFL/CFB rule and historical published evidence remain unchanged. No target empirical wrong-answer rate has been owner-approved.
