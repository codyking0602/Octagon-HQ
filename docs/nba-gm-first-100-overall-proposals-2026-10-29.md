## Targeted outlier reviews before release

- **Wembanyama 99 vs Jokić 98 and Shai 98**: compare multi-year excellence versus current two-way ceiling; 2025–26 box-plus-minus favors Jokić while current editorials increasingly favor Wembanyama. Keep 99 provisional pending film/impact review.
- **Kawhi 94, Tatum 94, Embiid 88, Anthony Davis 89, Kyrie 87, Haliburton 90, Damian Lillard 82, Ja Morant 81**: disentangle actual baseline ability from injury, unavailable seasons and comeback uncertainty. Do not permanently downgrade a player purely for games missed.
- **Jalen Johnson 90, Jamal Murray 90, Barnes 90, Bam 89, Chet 89**: verify calibrated 90/89 boundary using shared-current-impact standards, not All-Star label alone.
- **OG Anunoby 87, Caruso 81, Camara 80, Wallace 81, Ausar 83**: defender-heavy value deserves explicit evidence; avoid the opposite error of undervaluing off-ball defense.
- **2026 rookies AJ Dybantsa 77, Cameron Boozer 77**: only a temporary player evaluation. Their first few NBA games cannot establish long-term production. Revisit after a meaningful sample; keep game development upside distinct.
- **Ja Morant 81**: unusually large gap against reputation/old peak. Mandatory additional research; do not treat the number as certified.
